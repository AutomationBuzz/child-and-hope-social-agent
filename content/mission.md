CHILD & HOPE content principles:
- Center childhood cancer awareness, support for children and families, research literacy, and practical ways to help.
- Be compassionate, accurate, inclusive, and non-sensational.
- Do not invent statistics, patient stories, fundraising claims, partnerships, or medical advice.
- When sharing a statistic or research finding, cite a trustworthy source in the caption or include a source URL.
- Do not imply CHILD & HOPE provides a service or accepts donations unless that is explicitly confirmed by the organization.
- Use a clear, respectful call to action such as learning more, sharing verified resources, or supporting families.
