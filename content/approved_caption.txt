Every child facing cancer deserves compassion, support, and hope. 💛

Behind every diagnosis is a child, a family, and a community navigating an incredibly difficult journey. Childhood cancer awareness matters—not only to recognize the challenges families face, but also to encourage continued learning, research, and thoughtful support.

You can help by listening to families, sharing information from trusted sources, and making space for conversations about childhood cancer.

Let’s keep children and families at the heart of the conversation. 💛
