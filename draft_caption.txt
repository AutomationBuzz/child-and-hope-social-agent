Behind every statistic is a child and a family navigating uncertainty. The American Cancer Society estimates that about 9,680 children ages 0–14 in the United States will be diagnosed with cancer in 2026. This is an estimate, not a count of confirmed diagnoses, and childhood cancers differ by type—one number cannot describe every family’s experience.

Awareness means sharing reliable information, listening to families without assumptions, and supporting continued research into childhood cancers.

Learn more from the American Cancer Society: https://www.cancer.org/cancer/childhood-cancer/childhood-cancer-key-statistics.html
